SCM: endpoint-only, direct SQP, no planner

Current application conversion commands retain two anatomical endpoints. The isolated adapter calls the production SQP stage directly with the exact propagated samples. It does not call the planner, reject/remap the seed beforehand, or silently substitute a different path. Custom backend; 73 samples; clearance 0.002; caps of 1/4/24 accepted steps. The normal solver's rejected-trial limits and numerical checks remain.

Orange shapes are diagnostic candidates, not accepted production results: the third Fiber misses the solver's clearance check. Zero surface crossings does not establish bone clearance. Original projects and production algorithms remain unchanged.

Times are medians of two serial passes in reversed order, from prepared inputs. They exclude scene construction, document/UI scheduling and rendering; counts are advisory transverse triangle pairs, excluding coplanar contact/containment. All endpoint errors are zero and no degenerate triangles were found.
